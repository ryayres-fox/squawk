# Code of conduct

Short, because the project is small and the rule is not complicated.

**Be straight with people and argue about the work.** Say what you mean, say
when you are wrong, and give others the room to be wrong on the way to being
right. Technical disagreement is the point of a review; contempt is not an
argument, and neither is repeating yourself louder.

Not welcome here: personal attacks, harassment of any kind, demeaning remarks
about who someone is rather than what they wrote, and deliberately wasting a
maintainer's time.

**A report about someone's conduct goes the same way a security report does** —
GitHub's private vulnerability reporting on this repository, or a public issue
saying only that you need a private channel. It is read by the maintainer and
nobody else.

The maintainer decides what happens, from a word in the thread to a block, and
will say why. There is no committee; there is one person, and one person's
judgement is what this is.
